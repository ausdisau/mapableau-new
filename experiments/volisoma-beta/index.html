PURPOSE

Augmentation without surrendering autonomy.

Anthrogenisis is our proposed research philosophy: exploring how human capability and intelligent technology can become more closely coordinated, without reducing a person to a diagnosis or shifting control to a machine.

01

Multiple ways to express the same intention, including AAC, switches, gaze and future neural interfaces.

Innovative

Visible limits, human confirmation, cancellation, meaningful audit and independent review.

OUR COMMITMENTS

Your body. Your voice. Your decision.

We are interested in technology that follows a person's will and preferences, not systems that turn safety into unnecessary restrictions on everyday life.

Boundaries of this beta

Human-led: virtual actions require explicit confirmation.

Reversible: STOP ALL blocks further actions until reset.

Private: no sign-in, analytics, EEG data or health records.

Truthful: no physical robotics, clinical treatment or neural decoding.

Accessible: keyboard and screen-reader support is being tested against WCAG 2.2 AA; independent audit is pending.
