Our safeguards

Human authority comes first. Each proposed action requires meaningful consent; STOP interrupts immediately. Accessibility is never conditional on accepting AI augmentation.

No clinical or device claims. This release is a software-only simulation. No clinical trial, implanted interface, brain stimulation, therapeutic device, live EEG or motor actuation is provided.

Privacy by default. This static research simulator makes no network requests and stores no health information. It does not submit personal data or set tracking cookies.

Accessibility. Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Keyboard operation and reduced-motion preferences are supported; formal independent audit is pending.

Participate in co-design
