# DPO design inspiration (marketing UI)

Visual/UX cues drawn from Australian disability peak-body sites — colours stay MapAble navy/orange/royal/violet.

| Source | Patterns adopted |
|--------|------------------|
| [PDA](https://www.pda.org.au/) | Full-bleed hero with mission overlay; solid brand nav band; dual solid/ghost CTAs; pill buttons |
| [BCA](https://www.bca.org.au/) | High-contrast utility chrome; prominent accessibility entry; dark hero type hierarchy |
| [AFDO](https://afdo.org.au/) | Calm vision/mission statement pair; generous whitespace; rounded action chrome |
| [PWDA](https://pwd.org.au/) | Contact utility strip (phone/email); 3-up pathway tiles under hero; human-centred mission framing |

Out of scope: copying their brand colours, logos, or proprietary copy.
