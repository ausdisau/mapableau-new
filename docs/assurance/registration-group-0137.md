# Registration group 0137

Group **0137** (*Registration groups or classes of support*) covers NDIS digital platform providers. MapAble tracks inclusion — it does not assert registration outcome.

## Tracking

- `NdisRegistrationApplication.includes0137` — boolean flag
- `registrationGroups` array — must contain `"0137"` for digital platform pathway
- Audit scope: `buildRegistrationAuditScope()` includes 0137 applications

## Controls

NDIA digital platform framework controls (`ndia_digital_platform` kind) map to registration evidence requirements. See [control frameworks](./control-frameworks.md).

## Admin

`/admin/assurance/registration/0137`

**Disclaimers**

- Internal readiness ≠ certification, registration, or NDIA approval.
- Feature flags ≠ readiness. No AI agent may sign or approve production go-live.
- Including 0137 in an application record does not mean MapAble is registered as an NDIS digital platform provider.
