# Participant-Rights Constitution

All Mission Packs must preserve these rules. Encoded in contracts under `lib/mission-portfolio/contracts/participant-rights.ts`.

## Participant control

- The participant defines the goal and hard requirements.
- The participant controls disclosure.
- The participant may reject, pause, correct, revoke, dispute, or appeal.
- The participant may request a human.
- Delegates use their own identities; relationship does not equal authority.

## State honesty

Never collapse these distinctions:

discovered ≠ available ≠ compatible ≠ accepted ≠ confirmed ≠ delivered ≠ participant outcome  

Also: route found ≠ usable journey; payment completed ≠ service quality; course completed ≠ competency; persisted ≠ verified; venue declared ≠ independently confirmed; model candidate ≠ evidence; unknown ≠ accessible; stale ≠ current.

## AI boundaries

**AI may:** explain, summarise, draft, compare, suggest, simulate, identify missing information.

**AI must not independently:** change consent; approve delegates; assign workers; certify competency; prescribe equipment; give clinical treatment; approve claims or payments; close safeguarding matters; determine legal rights; submit to regulators; contact emergency services; declare a route safe; publish participant information.

## Prohibited scoring

- No participant worthiness, employability, loneliness, or burden score
- No universal accessibility score
- No single worker quality score
- No provider score influenced by payment
- No paid confidence or sponsored compatibility ranking

## Essential service access

- No smartphone-only, visual-only, QR-only, or biometric-only recovery pathway
- No app-response assumption for welfare or safety
