# MapAble Suburb Access Guide Content Standards

## Voice

Use plain language. The user may be planning an outing while tired, anxious, time-poor, or supporting another person. Write like a calm local guide, not a tourism brochure.

## Every guide must include

- Verification status
- Last updated date
- What is known
- What needs checking
- Transport notes
- Toilet notes or a clear statement that toilet information is not yet verified
- Parking/drop-off notes or a clear statement that the information is not yet verified
- Sensory or quiet-space notes
- Local planning risks
- Report update link
- Disclaimer

## Avoid

- "Fully accessible" unless verified and scoped.
- "Compliant" unless supported by an appropriate assessment.
- "Guaranteed" access language.
- Medical, legal, therapy, transport or NDIS advice claims.
- Generic tourism fluff that does not help planning.

## Good wording

- "Known accessible toilet data is still being checked."
- "Plan a fallback toilet stop before travelling."
- "This route may be busier during events."
- "Local verification is needed for kerb ramps and gradients."
- "This guide is practical access information, not a guarantee of access."

## Useful access questions

- Where is the nearest reliable accessible toilet?
- Is there a step-free route from transport or parking?
- Are kerb ramps present at key crossings?
- Are paths steep, uneven, narrow, busy, exposed or poorly lit?
- Is there a quiet place to reset?
- Are there seating/rest points?
- Is public transport usable for this trip?
- Does the suburb have heat, flood, crowd or event risks?
