# Controlled Pilot Design

## Actors

- Paid disability-led advisory participants
- One provider organisation
- Support workers
- One trainer / assessor
- One assistive-technology professional
- One equipment repairer
- One regional partner
- One developer sandbox partner
- Accessibility, privacy, and security reviewers

## Measures

- Communication comprehension and instruction accuracy
- Worker-readiness accuracy and missing-evidence behaviour
- Academy accessibility and competency-evidence quality
- Equipment continuity
- Offline mobile usability
- Participant outcome comprehension
- Provider operational burden
- Regional candidate quality
- Disclosure minimisation
- Partner sandbox safety
- Participant trust

## Kill criteria (do not progress when)

- Communication instructions mistaken for consent
- Supporters gain authority by relationship alone
- Opaque worker ranking from readiness
- Academy completion presented as competency
- Equipment matching implies clinical suitability
- Offline data unprotected
- Companion becomes compulsory
- Outcomes pressure positive reporting
- Providers can edit participant outcomes
- Provider Ops exposes excessive participant data
- Regional Capacity auto-assigns
- Partner APIs bypass purpose / field minimisation
- AI receives operational authority
- Rollback cannot be demonstrated
- Accessibility testing fails
