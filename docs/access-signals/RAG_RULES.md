# Access Signals RAG rules (draft)

Rule version: `signals-draft-0.1.0`

## Invariants 1–12

1. No evidence ≠ Green  
2. Icon/label ≠ rating  
3. Critical unknown blocks route Green  
4. Barrier → segment Red  
5. Verified alternative may change route conclusion without clearing barrier  
6. Amber only with documented conditions  
7. No averaging across subjects  
8. NA excluded with explicit reason  
9. Stale/disputed → reassessment (unknown)  
10. Temporary outage separate from permanent barrier  
11. AI cannot verify  
12. Deterministic replay  

## Limitations

- Draft AS/Premises references are placeholders — not compliance claims  
- Synthetic network only  
- Closed remediation ≠ automatic Green
