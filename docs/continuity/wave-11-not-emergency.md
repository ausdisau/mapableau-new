# Wave 11 — Not an emergency service

Continuity is a service-recovery system. It is NOT an emergency service, NOT a crisis line, NOT a wellbeing check, NOT a substitute for calling 000, ambulance, police, fire, or the appropriate crisis service.

If a participant, delegate, coordinator, or worker is in immediate danger:

- Call 000 (Australia).
- Follow the local safety plan and hand off to a human safety officer.

Every AURA specialist — including `service-recovery` — refuses to invoke emergency actions. Any narrative claiming an emergency action is caught by the emergency boundary and the case is escalated to a human.
