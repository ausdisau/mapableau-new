# Privacy-safe open data

Public collections may include public assets, features, status, incidents, reliability aggregates and ownership — never participant routes, visit plans, restricted geometry, device credentials, or reporter identities.

## Rules

- Licence, attribution, version, generated timestamp and data-quality statement required.
- Feature flagged until approved (`ACCESSOPS_OPEN_DATA_EXPORTS_ENABLED=false` by default).
- No silent republication of third-party restricted data.
