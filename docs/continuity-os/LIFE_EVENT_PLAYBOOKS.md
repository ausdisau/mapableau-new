# Recovery playbooks

Versioned definitions in `lib/continuity-os/recovery/playbooks.ts`.

## Initial playbooks

- `accessible_transport_cancellation` (first pilot)
- `support_worker_cancellation`
- `inaccessible_replacement_vehicle`
- `equipment_breakdown`
- `failed_hospital_to_home_handoff`
- `family_violence_safe_mode` (high-risk, human-only)

Playbooks guide response. They do not authorise action.

High-risk playbooks: route to authorised humans; minimise location; avoid default supporter contact; AURA never investigates or closes.
