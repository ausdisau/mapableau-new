# ContinuityOS Accessibility

Target: WCAG 2.2 AA.

- Keyboard-complete flows; visible focus; semantic headings
- Timeline and dependency **list** alternatives (no graph-only UI)
- No colour-only state; 200%/400% zoom; reflow; reduced motion
- Plain language; one question at a time on intake
- Critical terms always as visible text: failed, unknown, replacement, cost, approve, decline, cancel, contact, complaint, stop, emergency
- Stop Continuity control is easy to find and not paywalled
- No compulsory chat, map, or smartphone; SMS / printable / human call-back paths planned for supervised pilots
