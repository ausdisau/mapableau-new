# ContinuityOS accessibility

Target: WCAG 2.2 AA.

- Keyboard-complete flows
- Screen-reader labels and semantic headings
- Structured list alternative for every graph (`DependencyList`)
- No colour-only state
- Critical terms as visible text: failed, unknown, replacement, cost, approve, decline, cancel, contact, complaint, stop, emergency
- Stop Continuity control easy to find
- No compulsory chat, map or smartphone
- Offline/printable packs planned; SMS and human call-back supported in product copy
