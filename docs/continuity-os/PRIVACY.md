# ContinuityOS Privacy

- Life-event and recovery data are high sensitivity
- Collection minimisation; purpose limitation via RightsOS when merged
- Urgency does not bypass disclosure review
- No permanent dossier of every difficult life event — retention ceilings + deletion paths
- Family-violence safe mode: minimise notifications; no default supporter contact
- ContinuityOS friction ledger measures systems, not people
- Do not use ContinuityMetricSnapshot participant bands in ContinuityOS UX
