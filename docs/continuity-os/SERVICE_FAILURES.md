# Service failures

Signals are source-classified and unverified until assessed.

Classes describe services/environments — never participants:

AVAILABILITY · ACCESSIBILITY · TIMING · COMMUNICATION · HANDOFF · QUALITY_AND_SAFETY · FINANCIAL · DATA_AND_AUTHORITY · ENVIRONMENTAL

Severity ignores subscription tier, revenue and diagnosis.

Impact propagation preserves the prior dependency snapshot (`priorPlanSnapshotId` / new impact version) and requires participant review before consequential changes.
