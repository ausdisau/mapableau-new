# Disaster recovery operations

Operational procedures supporting [disaster recovery assurance](../assurance/disaster-recovery.md).

## Objectives

- RPO: 24 hours
- RTO: 72 hours

## Exercises

Record `IncidentResponseExercise` outcomes after tabletop or technical DR drills.

## Monitoring

- Admin: `/admin/assurance/continuity`
- Config: `DISASTER_RECOVERY_EXERCISES_ENABLED`

**Disclaimers**

- Internal readiness ≠ certification, registration, or NDIA approval.
- Feature flags ≠ readiness. No AI agent may sign or approve production go-live.
