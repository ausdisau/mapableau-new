# Business continuity operations

Operational procedures supporting [business continuity assurance](../assurance/business-continuity.md).

## Responsibilities

- Maintain backup configuration evidence on relevant assurance controls
- Record restore test dates when exercises complete
- Link continuity evidence to `CHG-01` and logging controls

## Monitoring

- Admin: `/admin/assurance/continuity`
- Flag: `DISASTER_RECOVERY_EXERCISES_ENABLED` (not readiness by itself)

**Disclaimers**

- Internal readiness ≠ certification, registration, or NDIA approval.
- Feature flags ≠ readiness. No AI agent may sign or approve production go-live.
