# Publication policy

Wave 13 publication surfaces are public-safe, append-only where appropriate and
bounded by strict redaction.

Never publish:

- Secrets, tokens, credentials or private keys.
- Prompts, chain-of-thought or exploit instructions.
- Participant identifiers, evidence bodies or appeal submissions.
- Fraud thresholds, risk thresholds or operational bypass details.

Always state:

- Register entry is not certification, endorsement or regulatory approval.
- How a person can challenge a consequential decision.
- Community recommendations are advisory by default.

Publication does not make automated regulator submissions and does not authorize
autonomous legal, clinical, safeguarding or financial decisions.
