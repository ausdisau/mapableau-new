# Appeals

Appeals provide a participant-safe challenge pathway for consequential decisions.

Requirements:

- Appeals must not retaliate against participants, advocates or reporters.
- Service access continues while reviewed where safe and lawful.
- The original decision-maker cannot decide the appeal.
- Reviewers must be independent and conflict checked.
- Grounds and evidence remain private participant data.
- Remedies are tracked when a decision is varied, remitted or overturned.

Public transparency pages may show aggregate appeal counts and process
information only. They must not publish appeal bodies, evidence or participant
identifiers.
