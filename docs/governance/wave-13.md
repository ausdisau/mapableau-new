# Wave 13 — Public-interest governance

Wave 13 makes consequential systems visible, challengeable and owner-accountable.
It adds governed system registration, public-safe algorithm register pages,
decision notices, appeals, independent review, community recommendations and
publication controls.

Mandatory principles:

- No opaque scoring.
- No automated regulator submissions.
- No autonomous legal, clinical, safeguarding or financial decisions.
- Register entry does not mean certification, endorsement or regulatory approval.
- Appeals must not retaliate against participants, advocates or reporters.
- Community recommendations are advisory by default.
- Public pages never expose secrets, prompts, participant data or operational abuse thresholds.

Primary surfaces:

- Public: `/transparency/*` and `/api/transparency/systems`.
- Participant: `/participant/appeals` and `/api/participant/decisions/*`.
- Admin: `/admin/governance/*` and `/api/admin/governance/*`.
- Community: `/api/governance/community`.
