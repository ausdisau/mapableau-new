# Guardian — AI Impact Assessment

**Status:** `DOCUMENTED_INTENT`  
**Not complete.** Do not treat as signed-off AIIA.

## Scope

Unified Care & Support Guardian (`lib/ai/platform/guardian/`) — deterministic control plane for privacy, safeguarding routing, and subordinated model signals.

## Intended benefits

- Fail-closed processing decisions
- Clear separation of model signals vs Core enforcement
- Auditable purpose / zone / processor choices

## Potential harms (initial)

- Over-blocking participant communication
- False-positive safeguarding cues causing distress
- Under-routing genuine safeguarding disclosures
- Privacy leakage via logging or external processors
- Paternalistic control framed as “safety”

## Model role

Models detect / classify / redact / explain / signal only. No statutory or SoR authority.

## Human oversight

Safeguarding, reportability, complaint/incident closure remain human-only.

## Residual risk

Requires privacy owner, safeguarding owner, and security review before any production flag enablement.

## Next evidence required

- Signed AIIA by privacy / product owners
- Bias and safeguarding evaluation results (Phase 9)
- Participant challenge journey tested
