# Decision notices

Decision notices explain consequential decisions in plain language, easy-read,
detailed and machine-readable forms.

Each notice must include:

- The decision and its effect on the person.
- Evidence-backed reasons.
- Known uncertainty or excluded considerations.
- How to challenge the decision.
- A machine-readable payload that excludes chain-of-thought.

Decision notices must not use opaque scores as reasons. They must not automate
regulator submissions or make autonomous legal, clinical, safeguarding or
financial decisions.
