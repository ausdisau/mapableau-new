# Offline

Local Vault may hold saved missions, offline visit packs, selected Access Passport view, emergency subset, equipment handling, trusted contacts, communication prefs, ledger summary, revocation queue, Stop AURA state.

Never store sensitive content in plain `localStorage`. Offline may draft disclosures but must not issue, renew, or replay approvals. Reconnect recompiles and requires fresh approval.

Flag: `MAPABLE_VAULT_LOCAL_STORAGE_ENABLED`.
