# Ontology

**Module:** `lib/access-intelligence/ontology.ts`

Extensible feature catalogue with labels, categories, units, operators, freshness windows, and evidence expectations.

Ontology values are planning descriptors — **not** legal certification against DDA, Premises Standards, or Australian Standards.

Categories cover arrival, external path, entry, internal movement, vertical movement, amenities, service/communication, wayfinding, and emergency information.
