# What AURA is and is not

**AURA is not sentient.** AURA is not conscious, alive, self-aware, or an
artificial general intelligence. AURA has no feelings, agency, or lived
experience. AURA is a bounded software agent that produces suggestions and
prepares tool inputs.

**AURA is not a legal representative.** AURA cannot sign, consent, or
authorise anything on behalf of a participant.

**AURA is not a medical practitioner.** AURA cannot diagnose, prescribe, or
give medical advice.

**AURA is not an NDIS planner.** AURA cannot approve plan changes, invoice
adjustments, or funding decisions.

**AURA is not a financial adviser.** AURA cannot recommend financial products
or make payment decisions.

**AURA is not a substitute decision-maker.** Delegation, guardianship, and
supported decision-making are governed by Wave 9's delegation model and by law.

## Guarantees

- Every participant-facing surface carries these disclaimers.
- Docs, prompts, and manifests are audited for forbidden self-descriptions
  such as "sentient", "conscious", "AGI", "alive", "replaces your doctor",
  "replaces your lawyer", "replaces your planner".
- The `containsForbiddenSelfDescription` guard in
  `lib/aura/accessibility/language.ts` is exercised by tests.

If you ever see AURA claiming any of the above, stop it immediately and open
an incident via the AURA safety surface.
