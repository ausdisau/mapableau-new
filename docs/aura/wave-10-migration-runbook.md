# Wave 10 migration runbook

**Wave 10 disclaimer:** AURA is not sentient, not a legal representative, not
a medical practitioner, not a financial adviser, and not a substitute
decision-maker. All participant data egress goes through Wave 9
`discloseParticipantData`. Wave 8 tenant context is required on every
mutation. A person still confirms the actions that affect a participant's
services, money, or safety.

See `docs/aura/wave-10-architecture-and-risk-plan.md` for the full
architecture, risk plan, and the list of prohibited actions.

## Scope

Wave 10 migration runbook. This surface is bounded by the AURA authority envelope, plan
validation, simulation, and approval mechanics. It cannot escalate its own
permissions, alter consent, or activate production integrations.
