# Wave 17 — Inclusive life planner

Wave 17 extends `ParticipationGoal` into participant-defined life planning and community participation. It does not create a parallel goal table, duplicate booking/calendar/access systems, or infer what a meaningful life should be.

Core rules:

- Participant wording is authoritative.
- Changing a goal is not failure.
- Discovery uses participant-approved filters only.
- Sponsored opportunities are separated, not ranked.
- Reflections are private by default and never exposed to organisers.
- Unknown or stale access information is not accessible.
- NDIS eligibility is never assumed.

Pack A Waves 14-16 are absent in this branch. Workforce allocation is represented by adapter stubs until Wave 16 lands.
