# MapAble Places · Plain-language participant information and consent draft
**Not ready to distribute: human research lead must approve contact details, compensation, storage, retention, withdrawal and ethics obligations first.**

## Invitation

We are building MapAble Places, a tool that helps people find out about accessibility at places they may visit.

We would like disabled people to help us test an early version. We want to understand whether it provides clear information, helps people ask useful questions, and lets people decide what information to share.

We are testing the **software, not you**.

## What will I do?

You may:
- look up access information about example places;
- tell us whether it seems clear or uncertain;
- write or edit questions for a place, using your preferred method;
- see a device share option without sending a real message;
- explore a fictional CripSquare community event;
- explain what worked and what you would change.

No real venue will receive a message. No attendance or location check-in is recorded. We will not ask for your medical records, diagnosis or home address.

The session is expected to take around 45–60 minutes, and you can take breaks or arrange shorter sessions.

## My access and communication choices

You can use your own keyboard, AAC, screen reader, switch access, enlarged text, voice input, or another method. You can answer by speech, typing, AAC, gestures or your preferred communication method. You can bring a supporter or interpreter if you wish. Your choices will not affect your payment.

Please tell the researcher what would make participation accessible. You do not have to tell us your diagnosis.

## Participation and payment

Participation is voluntary. You may skip tasks, ask for help, pause or stop at any point without giving a reason.

We will **not** record audio, video or your screen unless you separately choose to allow it. We will not add recordings or private study notes to a public GitHub repository.
