Do **not** score a human's disability, speed, intelligence, independent living potential or worth. Use these categories to identify product issues. A chosen supporter or AAC interface is not an error.

- **I** = completed with their chosen access method, without facilitator directing controls
- **A** = completed with assistance **chosen or requested** by the participant
- **B** = blocked by an interface barrier
- **D** = declined or stopped by participant
- **N** = not attempted / no comparable data

- **S** = distinguishes source description from a guarantee
