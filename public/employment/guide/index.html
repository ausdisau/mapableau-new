MediaHybrid · Part time

Community podcast producer

Plan episodes and turn interviews into clear, engaging stories.

Read role brief for community podcast producer
Skills needed at the start
Plan interview questions and organise a story. Community and volunteer projects are welcome examples.
Skills you can learn
Audio editing in the studio’s recording software.
Ways to show your work
A written episode outline, a transcript or a short audio sample.
Interview and workplace access
Written or AAC-supported interview available in this example. Step-free entry to the studio still needs confirmation.
DigitalRemote · Part time

Website testing assistant

Try everyday tasks on a website and explain what happened.

Read role brief for website testing assistant
Skills needed at the start
Follow a test scenario and describe the steps someone else could repeat.
Skills you can learn
Using an issue tracker and writing formal test cases.
Ways to show your work
Send a short written test report, annotated screenshots or a captioned recording.
Interview and workplace access
A typed interview is offered. Compatibility between the team’s tools and your assistive technology still needs checking.
CommunityOn-site · Casual

Library programs assistant

Help plan local events and prepare clear information for visitors.

Read role brief for library programs assistant
Skills needed at the start
Organise event tasks and communicate information in plain language.
Skills you can learn
Using the library’s event booking system.
Ways to show your work
Describe a community event you helped organise, or share an invitation you prepared.
Interview and workplace access
Questions can be supplied in advance. Confirm the entrance, accessible toilet and journey from the nearest stop before travelling.
