MapAble — Sensory-Friendly Canberra Half-Day Checklist
======================================================

Print or save this checklist before you travel.
This is practical planning support only. It is not medical, therapy, legal or NDIS advice.

Before you go
-------------
[ ] Check current opening hours for both venues
[ ] Check quiet-hour bookings (National Museum — first Tuesday of the month; confirm on the museum calendar)
[ ] Confirm accessible toilets / Changing Places needs
[ ] Confirm parking or pickup / drop-off details
[ ] Confirm transport for the Arboretum (there is NO direct public bus or light rail)
[ ] Pack headphones, sunglasses, fidgets, snacks, water and any communication cards
[ ] Agree an exit plan and a quieter backup venue if energy runs low
[ ] Allow buffer time between stops (avoid rushing)

Stop 1 — National Museum of Australia
-------------------------------------
[ ] Arrive during a quieter window if possible (or book quiet hours if available)
[ ] Note accessible parking near the main entrance (permit holders)
[ ] Locate lifts, accessible toilets and the Changing Places facility (Lower Ground)
[ ] Find the Faith and Quiet Room / multi-faith quiet space if needed
[ ] Choose one short gallery loop rather than “see everything”
[ ] Decide a rest spot (Gandel Atrium seating, outdoor cafe seating, quiet room)
[ ] Check wheelchair / scooter loan booking if you need it (suggest 24 hours ahead)

Stop 2 — National Arboretum Canberra
------------------------------------
[ ] Confirm your transport before leaving the museum (car, taxi, rideshare or booked accessible transport)
[ ] Use Village Centre accessible parking if applicable (permit holders — free)
[ ] Keep the visit short: Village Centre views, short sealed path, cafe or shade rest
[ ] Locate accessible toilets at the Visitors Centre
[ ] Avoid long hill walks if energy or mobility is limited
[ ] Watch for sun, wind and heat — paths can be exposed

Sensory preparation
-------------------
[ ] Noise: headphones / earplugs ready
[ ] Light: sunglasses or cap for outdoor glare
[ ] Crowds: plan for quieter rooms / outdoor space / early exit
[ ] Temperature: layers and water
[ ] Transitions: one clear cue for leaving each stop (“next stop is rest / transport”)

Low-transition alternatives (pick one if needed)
------------------------------------------------
[ ] Museum-only half day with longer rests
[ ] Arboretum-only short visit from accessible parking
[ ] Lake / National Triangle rest stop instead of a second venue
[ ] Cafe + outdoor seating only, then head home

Before travelling — confirm again
---------------------------------
Check current opening hours, quiet-hour bookings, transport availability and accessibility details before travelling.
